Taught pages

Pages you taught the filter to keep: pages similar to these survive ingest and the sweep. Teach from any page row ("Teach") or the popup's "Keep pages like this" button on a page that was not captured.

Blocked sites

The extension skips every page on a blocked site, including subdomains. The list is shared by the popup, this dashboard and the tray.

Auto-ignored sites

Sites whose captures the focus rules keep rejecting are ignored at the source once they cross the bar (10 rejections, zero keeps). One keep protects a site.
